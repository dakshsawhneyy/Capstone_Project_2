# Budget Management Strategy

## Monthly Budget Review

The Platform Team reviews cloud spending every month.

---

## Cost Monitoring

- AWS Cost Explorer
- Cost Breakdown by Service
- Cost Breakdown by Team
- Monthly Trend Analysis

---

## Budget Alerts

Alerts should be generated when cloud spending reaches:

- 50%
- 75%
- 90%
- 100%

of the monthly budget.

---

## Responsibilities

Platform Team

- Monitor cloud costs
- Optimize resource usage

Management

- Approve budgets
- Review monthly reports
