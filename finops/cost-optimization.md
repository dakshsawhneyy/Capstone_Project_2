# Cost Optimization Strategy

## Purpose

The objective of FinOps is to maximize business value while minimizing unnecessary cloud spending.

---

## Optimization Areas

- Right-sizing compute resources
- Remove unused resources
- Enable Auto Scaling
- Monitor idle workloads
- Optimize storage usage

---

## Monthly Review

- Compute Costs
- Storage Costs
- Network Costs
- Kubernetes Costs

---

## Goals

- Reduce unnecessary spending
- Improve resource utilization
- Increase cost visibility
